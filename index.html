EVA — a private personal assistant

EVA is a personal assistant built by Riyan Khan for his own use. It runs on Riyan's private server and is used from his own phone and Mac. It is not a public service: there are no sign-ups and no other users.

What EVA does

  • Answers questions and helps with study and planning (English, Hindi, Hinglish).
  • Reminders, weather, travel time and trip planning.
  • With Riyan's permission, helps with his own Google account: summarises his email, writes drafts, sends an email only after he approves it, shows and adds calendar events, manages his Google Tasks to-dos, and saves notes to his Google Drive.
  • A short morning brief and night recap.
